Unit 3: Websites

Website purpose, analytics tools and development process steps.

1. Types and Purposes of Websites

Categorización de sitios según su objetivo principal (pág. 20):

  • Personal: To provide information about an individual.
  • Commercial/Company: To sell products or provide services.
  • Organisational: To inform about an idea or event.
  • News: To obtain information / share international news.
  • Social Networking: To exchange personal information.
  • Educational: To share knowledge and enable online learning.
Key Verbs: offer, practise, present, promote, sell, share.
2. Vocabulary: Website Analytics

Términos utilizados para medir el rendimiento de una web (pág. 22):

Traffic Movement and actions of visitors.

Meta tag Invisible information (hidden keywords).

Pageviews Total number of pages viewed.

Bounce rate Percentage of visitors who view only one page.

Time on site How long visitors spend on the website.

User profile Information about a user and their browsing sites.

3. Grammar: Describing Steps (Sequencing)

Para describir el orden de las acciones, utilizamos conectores específicos (pág. 24):

Beginning:
First / To start
Continuous:
Next / Then / After that
End:
Finally / To finish

IT Development Workflow:

  1. First, discuss requirements with the customer.
  2. Next, create a website specification.
  3. Then, design and develop the website.
  4. After that, give the project to programmers.
  5. Finally, test the website.

Gramática Esencial y Perfiles IT

Unit 1: Working in the IT industry (Pearson Longman)

1. Presentarse y conocer gente

Para presentarse profesionalmente se utilizan estructuras directas y cordiales:

I am... / My name is... (Yo soy / Mi nombre es)
Nice to meet you / Pleased to meet you (Gusto en conocerte)
Welcome to the team (Bienvenido al equipo)

Verb "To Be" (Ser o Estar): Es la base para dar información personal.

Afirmativo Contracción Interrogativo
I am I'm Am I...?
You are You're Are you...?
He/She/It is He's / She's / It's Is he/she/it...?
We/They are We're / They're Are we/they...?
2. Perfiles y Roles (Jobs in IT)

El material identifica roles específicos y sus funciones técnicas:

Software Developer

Designs and develops applications/games.

System Analyst

Solves computer problems and analyses user needs.

Database Administrator

Manages, analyses, and presents electronic data.

Network Architect

Plans and designs network structures.

IT Support Officer

Ensures all computers and peripherals work properly.

Network Administrator

Sets up firewalls, passwords, and security levels.

3. Presente Simple: Rutinas y Permanencia

Se utiliza para hablar de lo que hacemos habitualmente o cosas que siempre son ciertas.

Question: What do you do?
Answer: I work for IBM in Poland.
He/She: Sigue la regla de agregar una 's' al final del verbo.
Example: He works as a web developer.

Para negaciones se utilizan los auxiliares Don't y Doesn't.

4. Alfabeto y Acrónimos (Spelling)

En el mundo IT, es fundamental saber deletrear nombres técnicos y acrónimos clave:

HTML
HyperText Markup Language
FTP
File Transfer Protocol
WLAN
Wireless Local Area Network

Question style: "How do you spell that?" / "What does HTML stand for?"

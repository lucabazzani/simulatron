1. Database Basics & Data Processing

Una base de datos almacena, permite acceso y gestiona registros (pág. 29). El procesamiento de datos sigue un orden lógico (pág. 30):

  1. Data collection: Gather raw data.
  2. Data sorting: Create categories and relevant groups.
  3. Data entry: Enter information into the system.
  4. Data validation: Check for faults and inconsistencies.
  5. Data tabulation: Format and arrange for analysis.

Polite Requests:

- Could you help me, please?
- Would you explain how to do that?
2. Grammar: Present Perfect for Reporting

Usamos Present Perfect para hablar de acciones recientes que tienen un resultado en el presente (pág. 53).

Have/Has + Past Participle
  • Positive: I have unplugged the computer.
  • Negative: She hasn't finished the report.
  • Question: Have you checked the cable connections?
3. Troubleshooting & Toolkits

Vocabulario clave para resolver incidentes de hardware (pág. 56):

  • Multimeter: Measures electricity.
  • Hex key: For hexagonal sockets.
  • Tweezers: To hold small objects.
  • Pliers: To cut or bend materials.
  • Cable Tester: Checks connections.

Fault States: disconnected, unplugged, switched off, noisy, blank screen.

4. Security: Threats vs. Solutions
Threats (pág. 60):
  • Virus: Can copy itself and infect.
  • Trojan: Usually free but malicious.
  • Spyware: Affects user privacy.
  • Worm: Spreads without user action.
Solutions (pág. 61):
  • Firewall: Perimeter protection.
  • SSL: Secure Socket Layer.
  • 2FA: Two-factor authentication.
  • Encryption: For sensitive data.
5. Workplace Rules: Prohibitions

Cómo expresar reglas estrictas y prohibiciones en la oficina (pág. 65):
